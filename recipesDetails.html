About Recipe

Introduction

Gajar ka halwa is more than a sweet treat. It feels warm on winter days and fills homes with happiness. Fresh red carrots become sweetest during winter season, while rich milk and pure desi ghee create authentic traditional flavor.

Cooking Process

Step-wise Recipe

1

Prepare Carrots

Peel and wash fresh carrots carefully. Grate them using medium holes for perfect texture.

2

Cook with Milk

Add carrots and milk into a heavy pan. Cook slowly while stirring continuously.

3

Add Sugar & Ghee

Once milk reduces, add sugar and pure desi ghee. Stir gently until mixture becomes rich and glossy.

4

Final Finish

Add mawa, cardamom and dry fruits. Serve warm and enjoy authentic festive sweetness.

Pro Cooking Tips

Tips to Make Perfect Gajar Halwa

Use fresh red carrots for naturally sweet flavor and vibrant color.

Slow cooking creates creamy texture and rich authentic taste.

Medium heat works best to avoid burning and maintain softness.

Common Questions

FAQs

Can I make this without mawa?

Yes, you can use extra milk and cook longer for creamy texture.

How long does it stay fresh?

Store in refrigerator for 3-4 days in airtight container.

Which carrots work best?

Fresh red winter carrots provide the best authentic flavor.

Our Journey

Three Decades of Pure Excellence

From a single village collection centre to a nationwide dairy powerhouse — here's how we grew.

1991

The Humble Beginning

Founded with a single milk collection centre in Gujarat, Madhusudan began its journey with a mission to bring farm-fresh dairy to urban households.

🌾
🏭
1998

First Processing Plant

Established our first state-of-the-art processing plant, enabling us to scale production while maintaining ISO-level hygiene and quality standards.

2005

ISO 22000:2005 Certification

Achieved prestigious ISO 22000:2005 certification — a landmark that cemented Madhusudan's commitment to international food safety and quality management systems.

🏅
🚛
2012

Pan-India Cold Chain Network

Launched a pan-India cold chain logistics network, enabling fresh dairy delivery across 5 Lakh+ retail outlets and expanding our farmer network to over 3 Lakh members.

2020

Digital Transformation

Introduced digital milk testing systems and automated farmer payment platforms, achieving transparent 10-day payment cycles and modernising 500+ milk collection routes.

📱
🚀
Today

India's Trusted Dairy Icon

100+ product variants, millions of happy families, and a future built on innovation, sustainability, and India's farmers.
